    <div class="about">
      <p>I am a <strong>Postdoctoral Researcher</strong> at <a href="https://scads.ai/"><strong>ScaDS.AI</strong></a>, <a href="https://tu-dresden.de/"><strong>Technische Universität Dresden</strong></a>, working with <a href="https://faerber-lab.github.io/">Prof. Michael Färber</a>. My research is on multilingual NLP, AI safety, and mechanistic interpretability, with the goal of building LLMs that stay reliable, truthful, and safe across languages.</p>
      <p>I completed my Ph.D. at <a href="https://iitgn.ac.in/"><strong>IIT Gandhinagar</strong></a> with <a href="https://mayank4490.github.io/">Prof. Mayank Singh</a>, on assessing factuality, toxicity, and safety in LLMs. During the Ph.D. I worked with <a href="https://www.tomhartvigsen.com/">Prof. Thomas Hartvigsen</a> at the <a href="https://www.virginia.edu/"><strong>University of Virginia</strong></a> on multilingual and interpretable content moderation, showing that English-only safety systems fail to generalize, and with <a href="https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/susitara/">Dr. Sunayana Sitaram</a> at <a href="https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-india/"><strong>Microsoft Research India</strong></a> on why multilingual benchmarks hide performance disparities.</p>
